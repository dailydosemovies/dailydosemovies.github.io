The Prestige Ending Explained – The Secret Behind the Magic

The Prestige, directed by Christopher Nolan, is a psychological thriller about rivalry, obsession, and sacrifice. The film follows two magicians, Robert Angier and Alfred Borden, who compete to create the ultimate illusion. The ending reveals shocking truths about both characters.

Borden’s Secret

Throughout the film, Borden performs a trick called “The Transported Man.” The secret is that Borden has an identical twin brother. They share one life, switching places constantly to maintain the illusion. This explains why Borden behaves differently at times.

Angier’s Method

Angier uses Nikola Tesla’s machine to create clones of himself. Each time he performs the trick, he creates a new clone and kills the original or the clone. This reveals Angier’s willingness to sacrifice himself repeatedly.

The Meaning of the Ending

The ending shows Angier’s obsession destroyed him, while Borden survived through sacrifice and discipline. The film explores how obsession can consume a person’s identity.

Conclusion

The Prestige reveals that true magic comes with sacrifice. Both characters gave up their personal lives to achieve greatness.
