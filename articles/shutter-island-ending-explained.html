Shutter Island Ending Explained – Truth Behind Teddy Daniels

Shutter Island, directed by Martin Scorsese, is a psychological thriller that explores trauma and denial. The story follows Teddy Daniels, a U.S. Marshal investigating a disappearance at a mental institution.

The Major Twist

Teddy Daniels is actually Andrew Laeddis, a patient at the institution. His investigation is part of a therapeutic role-play designed to help him accept reality.

Why Andrew Created Teddy

Andrew created the Teddy persona to escape guilt over his wife’s actions and her death.

The Final Scene Meaning

Andrew appears to relapse but may actually choose to undergo treatment voluntarily, suggesting acceptance.
